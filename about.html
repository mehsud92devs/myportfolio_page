Abdul Majeed is a dedicated SEO specialist with over five years of hands-on experience helping businesses of all sizes improve their search engine visibility and drive sustainable organic traffic. With a deep understanding of how search engines work and what users are looking for, Abdul builds strategies that deliver measurable, long-term results.

From comprehensive technical audits to topical authority content strategies, Abdul's approach covers every layer of modern SEO. My Approach

SEO is not about gaming algorithms or finding shortcuts. It is about making websites genuinely better for both search engines and the real people who use them. Abdul approaches every project by first establishing a deep understanding of the client's business, their target customers, their competitors, and the current state of their website through a thorough baseline audit.

From that foundation, a prioritized roadmap is built that addresses the highest-impact opportunities first. Progress is tracked through consistent reporting using Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, and rank tracking tools. Every recommendation is backed by data, every result is measured and reported transparently.
