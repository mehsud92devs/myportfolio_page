Professional SEO Services

Data-driven strategies that improve rankings, drive organic traffic, and grow your business online.

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO is the foundation of any successful search strategy. It covers every element within your web pages that influences how search engines understand and rank your content. Abdul's on-page SEO service begins with in-depth keyword research to identify the exact terms your target audience uses, followed by strategic placement across page titles, meta descriptions, header tags, and body content.

The process also includes a thorough content gap analysis to identify topics your site is missing, content restructuring for improved E-E-A-T signals, internal linking optimization to distribute page authority, URL structure improvements, image optimization including alt text and file naming, and alignment of each page with its primary search intent. The result is pages that search engines can confidently rank and users find genuinely useful.

What You Get

  • Full keyword research and mapping for all target pages
  • Meta title and description optimization for every page
  • Header tag hierarchy audit and restructuring
  • Internal linking strategy and implementation
  • Content gap analysis and optimization recommendations
Best for: Businesses with existing websites looking to improve current rankings

Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO determines how authoritative and trustworthy your website appears to search engines based on signals from across the web. The most important of these signals is the quality and quantity of websites linking to yours. Abdul builds white-hat link profiles through careful outreach, editorial placements, digital PR campaigns, and niche-specific guest posting.

Every link acquisition campaign starts with competitor backlink analysis to identify proven link sources, followed by prospecting, personalized outreach, and content creation that earns genuine editorial links. The service also includes brand mention monitoring to convert unlinked mentions into backlinks, anchor text diversification to maintain a natural profile, and disavowal of any toxic links harming your rankings.

What You Get

  • Competitor backlink gap analysis and opportunity report
  • White-hat link building outreach and placements
  • Guest posting on relevant, high-authority publications
  • Brand mention monitoring and link conversion
  • Toxic backlink identification and disavowal file
Best for: Businesses in competitive niches that need to build domain authority

Local SEO

Local SEO ensures that when potential customers in your area search for the products or services you offer, your business appears prominently. This includes ranking in the Google Maps pack, which drives significant call and foot traffic for location-based businesses. Abdul's local SEO service covers every aspect of local search visibility.

The process begins with a thorough Google Business Profile audit and optimization covering all categories, attributes, photos, Q and A, and post cadence. It continues with local citation building to establish NAP consistency across hundreds of directories, local keyword research targeting geo-specific search terms, review management strategies to build social proof, local schema markup implementation, and geo-targeted content creation to dominate neighborhoods and surrounding areas.

What You Get

  • Google Business Profile full optimization and management
  • Local citation building across top directories
  • NAP consistency audit and correction
  • Local keyword research and geo-targeted content
  • Local schema markup and map pack optimization
Best for: Local service businesses, restaurants, retail stores, and clinics

Technical SEO

Technical SEO addresses the infrastructure of your website to ensure search engines can efficiently crawl, render, and index all of your content. Without a solid technical foundation, even the best content and strongest backlink profile will underperform. Abdul conducts comprehensive technical audits using professional tools including Screaming Frog, Google Search Console, and PageSpeed Insights to identify and prioritize every issue.

Service coverage includes crawl error resolution and redirect management, XML sitemap optimization, robots.txt configuration, Core Web Vitals improvement targeting LCP, FID, and CLS, HTTPS and SSL verification, structured data and schema markup implementation, canonical tag strategy, hreflang for multilingual sites, JavaScript rendering analysis, duplicate content resolution, and page speed optimization for both mobile and desktop environments.

What You Get

  • Comprehensive technical SEO audit with priority recommendations
  • Core Web Vitals improvement plan and implementation support
  • Schema markup implementation for rich results eligibility
  • Crawl budget optimization and sitemap configuration
  • Page speed optimization report and action items
Best for: All websites, especially those with large page counts or technical debt

AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring your content to appear as the direct answer to user queries in featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, voice search results, and Google's rich result formats. As search behavior shifts toward zero-click results, capturing position zero becomes a critical traffic source for authoritative brands.

Abdul's AEO service covers comprehensive featured snippet opportunity research, question-based content strategy development, content reformatting for direct answer capture, FAQ schema and HowTo schema implementation, structured data for rich results, voice search keyword targeting, and PAA optimization. Each tactic is designed to make your content the source that search engines choose to surface at the very top of the results page, above traditional blue links.

What You Get

  • Featured snippet opportunity audit and targeting list
  • Question-based content creation and optimization
  • FAQ and HowTo schema markup implementation
  • People Also Ask strategy and content alignment
  • Voice search keyword research and content formatting
Best for: Informational sites, service businesses, and brands targeting voice search

GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

Generative Engine Optimization is the emerging discipline of optimizing your brand and content to be cited, referenced, and recommended by AI-powered search platforms including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, and Bing Copilot. As generative AI reshapes how users discover information, being cited by these systems is the new frontier of organic visibility.

Abdul's GEO approach focuses on entity optimization and knowledge graph building, authoritative content structuring for AI citation, brand entity establishment across the web, semantic SEO that aligns with how large language models understand topics, and structured data that helps AI systems correctly identify and categorize your brand. This service is ideal for businesses that want to lead in the next generation of search.

What You Get

  • Entity optimization and brand knowledge graph setup
  • AI citation optimization for major generative platforms
  • Semantic SEO content structuring for LLM comprehension
  • Authoritative content strategy for AI reference
  • GEO performance monitoring and reporting framework
Best for: Brands wanting to appear in AI-powered search answers and citations

Topical Map Creation

A topical map is a comprehensive blueprint of all the content your website needs to publish in order to become the recognized authority on a given subject in the eyes of both search engines and users. Modern SEO rewards websites that cover topics in depth and breadth, rather than those targeting individual keywords in isolation.

Abdul builds topical maps by first identifying the core topics and subtopics within your niche, then structuring them into pillar pages and supporting content clusters connected through strategic internal linking. The deliverable includes a competitor topical coverage analysis revealing gaps in what they have published, a complete content architecture diagram, a prioritized publishing roadmap, and internal linking blueprints. This service provides a 12-to-18-month content foundation designed to compound in authority over time.

What You Get

  • Full topical authority map for your niche
  • Pillar page and content cluster architecture
  • Competitor topical coverage gap analysis
  • Internal linking blueprint for the entire cluster
  • Prioritized 12-month publishing roadmap
Best for: New sites and businesses wanting to build long-term authority

Website Structure and Architecture

A well-planned website structure ensures that search engines can crawl every important page efficiently, understand how your content relates to itself, and distribute ranking signals across your domain. Poor site architecture is one of the most common yet overlooked reasons why websites fail to rank despite having quality content and backlinks.

Abdul's website structure service covers information architecture planning from the ground up, URL structure design that supports both user navigation and crawl efficiency, navigation hierarchy optimization, silo structure implementation for topic clustering, breadcrumb navigation setup, crawl depth reduction to bring important pages within three clicks of the homepage, and internal link equity distribution planning to ensure your strongest pages support the pages that need rankings most.

What You Get

  • Full information architecture audit and redesign plan
  • URL structure optimization recommendations
  • Silo structure implementation for topic relevance
  • Breadcrumb navigation setup and schema markup
  • Internal link equity map and distribution strategy
Best for: Large websites, e-commerce stores, and sites undergoing redesign

Keyword Research

Keyword research is the compass that directs every other SEO effort. Without knowing exactly what terms your audience searches for, with what intent, and at what competition level, even well-executed strategies can miss the mark. Abdul's keyword research service builds a complete keyword universe tailored to your business, industry, and target audience.

The process uses Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Keywords Everywhere to identify search volume, keyword difficulty, click-through rates, and trends. Deliverables include a comprehensive keyword universe spreadsheet, search intent classification for every keyword, long-tail opportunity identification where you can win faster, competitor keyword gap analysis revealing terms they rank for but you do not, keyword clustering and grouping for content planning, and separation of commercial, informational, and navigational keywords to guide content strategy.

What You Get

  • Full keyword universe spreadsheet with metrics
  • Search intent classification for every keyword
  • Competitor keyword gap analysis report
  • Keyword clusters grouped by topic and intent
  • Priority keyword list with difficulty and opportunity scores
Best for: New sites, rebrands, and campaigns targeting new markets
